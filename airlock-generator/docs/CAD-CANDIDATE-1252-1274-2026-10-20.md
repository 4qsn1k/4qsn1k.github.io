## Access control and 10-second blocking — user clarification

The only access-controlled entry is **CNC → 1257**, authorized by
**Bolid S2000-2**. This door is the sole door whose reader and
Bolid BUSY coordination are relevant to the airlock system.
Its exact terminal assignment, contact polarity and reverse
egress behavior have not been verified.

Every other controlled doorway (the **six** internal door edges
from 1257 through 1252) belongs to **local airlock automation,
without separate access-control card readers or S2000-2 BUSY**.
The operator specified **10-second timed blocking**, correcting
the earlier assumption of an ordinary `mutual` (instant release)
policy. The first CNC entry is **also a physical member of group
1257**, although its authorization is performed by S2000-2.

The operator explicitly confirmed the following **door-group algorithm**:

1. While one door is OPEN, the OTHER doors in its incident room
   group(s) are blocked for as long as the door remains open.
   An opened door gets no self-block request (it can be closed),
   and its logical GREEN is denied.
2. After the last open door of each incident group is CONFIRMED
   CLOSED, that group's independent **10-second hold** starts.
   During the hold **all doors in that group remain inhibited**,
   including the one just closed.
3. An opening during the hold **cancels the timer**; after closing
   again, the full 10 seconds must pass before release.
4. On a shared door the two independent incident-group requests
   are combined by logical OR. A closed inhibited door does not
   propagate a new open condition to a remote group.
5. The automatic hold timer itself is **not** a ventilation
   completion acknowledgement or a command to an air shower.
   Actual technical purge, ventilation and pressure interlocks
   require separately specified controls.

The candidate metadata now records this as `timingMode=timed-hold`,
`timedBlockHoldSec=10` and `timedBlockStartEvent=ALL_ZONE_DOORS_CLOSED_STABLE`
in every room group. All seven physical doors participate in these
group timers; only the **six internal doors lack SCUD readers**.
The S2000-2 BUSY contact and physical relays remain unassigned.

**Offline implementation note:** the existing reference
`AirlockStTestbench` has a post-close timer under its legacy
`purge` mode. The test translates `timed-hold` into this legacy
state machine with `purgeSec=10` and
`ventilationControlMode=independent`. This is a *reference
simulation only*; it neither runs ventilation nor compiles a
user-ready native Owen Logic FBD project. It does not imply that
an independent actual 30-second air purge (if required for this
cleanroom) is satisfied after ten seconds.

One technical implementation detail in the current reference
model is an 80-ms closed-contact confirmation and a 20-ms scan
approximation; therefore 10 seconds is measured after confirmed
group closure, not by a calibrated physical stopwatch.
