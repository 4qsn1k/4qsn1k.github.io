## Required three-layer model

1. Physical **rooms** carry identifiers, class, pressure and CAD
   geometry. Some rooms may be outside the controlled system.
2. Physical **doors** carry exactly one ID, one reed, the two actual
   endpoint spaces, optional lock and reader, and actuator polarity.
3. Independent **interlock zones** are the specific sets of door IDs
   that must not simultaneously admit passage. A shared physical
   doorway can be a member of **two adjacent zones**, without
   duplicating its sensor, actuator or terminal assignment.

Example, **purely illustrative, NOT THIS CAD TOPOLOGY**:

Zone S1 includes D1, D2 and D3. Zone S2 includes D3, D4 and D5.
Opening D1 must inhibit only D2/D3; D4/D5 do not inherit a remote
inhibit transitively. Opening shared D3 must inhibit D1/D2 and
D4/D5. Inhibited-but-closed D3 must not be treated as an opened reed,
or the two zones would incorrectly inhibit every door in the complex.

For an opened door, its GREEN permission is denied; hardware lock
state is evaluated separately and must not prevent a fire-safe exit.
When all participating doors are closed, each incident zone applies
its own `mutual` (release without purge) or `purge` (timer) policy.
Any reopened door restarts a purge when specified by that zone.
External APS hardware/egress safety has **separate independent
authority** from software Modbus/PLC interlocks.

## Implementation and acceptance

The existing `fbd-generator.js` and PR #25 already model physical
doors with two endpoint room IDs, one reed, local nontransitive
interlocks, and OR of the two incident room's inhibit requests.
The existing model is **room-centric**. A fully independent *zone*
abstraction, where a physical door belongs to two operator-created
interlock zones, still needs an explicit schema, resolver and tests.
Do not claim this new capability is already implemented.

Before building a native FBD from this crop, the operator must
confirm the actual controlled door inventory and how the doors are
grouped into interlock zones. Once confirmed, add one unique physical
door record per real doorway, zone membership, purge/timeout policy,
and hardware mapping, then test:
- shared doorway opening inhibits only the incident zones;
- no transitive cascade through closed shared doors;
- single reed and single physical output per shared doorway;
- independent purge timers, simultaneous-door faults, fire priority
  and loss-of-network safe behavior;
- correct electrical safe state per individual lock hardware type.
