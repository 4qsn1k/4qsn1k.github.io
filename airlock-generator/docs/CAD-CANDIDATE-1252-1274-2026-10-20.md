inhibited **must not be interpreted as an open reed**, and so must
not propagate a transitive cascade. In particular, opening the
entry CNC↔1257 **does not directly inhibit doors past 1256/1255**.

At convergence into 1252, opening either 1254↔1252 or 1253↔1252
also inhibits the other entrance to 1252, while leaving remote
doors on the nonincident branches unchanged.

## Access control and 10-second blocking — user clarification

The only access-controlled entry is **CNC → 1257**, authorized by
**Bolid S2000-2**. This door is the sole door whose reader and
Bolid BUSY coordination are relevant to the airlock system.
Its exact terminal assignment, contact polarity and reverse
egress behavior have not been verified.

Every other controlled doorway (the **six** internal door edges
from 1257 through 1252) belongs to **local airlock automation,
without separate access-control card readers or S2000-2 BUSY**.
The operator specified **10-second blocking** for these transitions,
explicitly correcting the earlier assumption of an ordinary
`mutual` (instant release) policy.

The candidate metadata now records `timedBlockHoldSec=10` on
the six internal door records, `cardReaderDoorId=D_CNC_1257`,
and an unresolved `timedBlockingRequirement`. It deliberately
does **not** choose whether the 10-second interval starts when a
reed opens, when that door closes, or from a separately authorized
request. The behavior of a door left open and of re-opening during
the interval is also unconfirmed.

**Do not implement a PLC timing circuit from this setting yet.**
The existing incident-room open-door inhibit matrix below is a
logic-model QA from the previous policy, **not evidence that
the user approved it as the precise deployed 10-second algorithm**.
The previous automated mutual-mode tests continue to show that the
physical topology can be connected to a generator, but they DO NOT
qualify the new 10-second blocking behavior.
