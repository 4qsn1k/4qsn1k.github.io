## First confirmed interlock group Z_R1257

| Logical physical door ID | Endpoint A | Endpoint B | Known condition |

| D_CNC_1257 | CNC | 1257 | Entrance from CNC / old CNC–D |
| D_1257_1256 | 1257 | 1256 | Passage from 1257 into 1256 |
| D_1257_1255 | 1257 | 1255 | Passage from 1257 into 1255 |

The three records represent **three distinct physical doors**,
each with exactly one reed sensor and at most one physical actuator.
The older CNC–D proof-of-concept was a two-door test, but the
**real group 1257 must allow three doors**.

The operator **explicitly confirmed**:
opening **D_CNC_1257** must inhibit opening **D_1257_1256** and
**D_1257_1255**, while the CNC door is itself allowed to close.
In the candidate record this is the `confirmedRules` entry.

**Proposed symmetric design rule** (not separately commissioned):
opening D_1257_1256 denies D_CNC_1257 and D_1257_1255,
and opening D_1257_1255 denies D_CNC_1257 and D_1257_1256.
This follows the room-centric interlock policy and is verified by
the isolated test model, but commissioning needs the operator's
approval of reader placement, locks, release behavior and the
fire/egress chain.

Once an incident door closes, subsequent release follows the
**site-selected mode** for the zone: `mutual` or `purge`.
The real mode and period have not been specified for this location.
No new 30-second value is silently assigned. A separate
`mutual` policy is used **only within the offline connectivity test**,
not as site configuration.

## Following sections of the chain

1256 and 1255 branch from the first airlock and lead toward cleaner
zones, but **their exact subsequent controlled door IDs, membership
and interlock group assignments are not yet established**.

A physical doorway connecting two controlled groups shall be
defined **once**, its single reed fanned out to both groups, and
the two independent blocking requests combined by OR. The
condition "this door is inhibited" is not the same as "its
reed indicates open": an inhibited-but-closed door must not
propagate a block to a downstream group. Nonincident groups
remain independent. The source FBD generator already supports
room-centric incident-door blocking, but not general arbitrary
overlapping zone authoring.
