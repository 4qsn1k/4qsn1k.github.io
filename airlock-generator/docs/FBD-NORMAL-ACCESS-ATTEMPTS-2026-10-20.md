# Visual FBD bench: normal access request without S2000-2 BUSY

Date: 2026-10-20. **Offline simulation only.**
No real controller, dry contact, lock or reader is switched.

## Operator-selected architecture

The CNC→1257 entrance has exactly one S2000-2 reader. The six
other confirmed doors use local normal access requests. There are
seven unique reed inputs and six 10-second room timers.

**Normal request eligibility is a two-condition AND**:

1. The person's fresh request (Bolid card at CNC entry, local
   button/request elsewhere) occurs at the tested moment.
2. The physical door's incident room(s) are ready and all
   its reed inputs are closed. The readiness timers are
   generated by the existing FBD gate netlist.

This is a **logical acceptance TEST** only; no lock coil,
relay pulse or physical unlock is synthesized.

### Crucial replay regression

Suppose a card is presented at t=4 s while the room's 10-second
hold is still active. The request must remain DENIED. When the
timer becomes ready at t=10 s, the old event must **not
automatically replay** and start an unlock. A *new* card or button
event after readiness is required.

This is verified in:
- `normal-access-event-qa.js`, which calls the FBD netlist
  simulator and records independent access-event decisions.
- `tests/normal-access-event-qa.test.js` (9/9 PASS).
- `scripts/render-timed-fbd-viewer.js`, with separate
  `Проверить карту` / `Запрос кнопки` event buttons.
- `tests/fbd-interactive-viewer.test.js` (8/8 PASS), including
  refusal before the hold, no automatic later acceptance, and
  distinct calculations for BUSY and no-BUSY alternatives.

The selector `Интеграция СКУД` has a physical BUSY choice and
the operator's new no-BUSY lock-command permission variant.
In the new variant the live FBD display labels `busy` as
**logical room occupancy only**, not a physical Bolid BUSY DO.

## Engineering resource comparison

With **seven physical doors, one buzzer, per-door single
relay red/green traffic control, and DI for APS and reset**:

| Normal access integration | DI | Relay DO | External BUSY |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Own lock permission relay AND Bolid K1 (operator proposal) | 9 | 15 | 0 |
| S2000-2 normal lock relay plus external Busy (old variant) | 9 | 16 | 1 |

An independent common alarm relay adds 1 DO. Buttons wired
as real PLC inputs increase DI; the current standard estimate
does not assume seven extra button inputs automatically.
A two-channel Bolid may share one BUSY electrically but
cannot use that one wire for two independent 10s timer domains.

Two DO of design reserve are included in the preliminary
automatic controller choice; selection requires confirmation
of real device modification, relay contact ratings, actual
physical I/O wiring and life-safety design.

## Explicitly NOT yet built or qualified

- **Hold-open command** or actuation pulse duration for the
  physical lock. The event acceptance is NOT a real
  lock-release pulse, does not keep the lock released
  through an open/close movement, and must not be wired
  directly to a real lock.
- Actual timing/acknowledgment of Bolid K1 access grant.
  Bolid might signal card access permitted even though
  the external PLC contact denies normal unlock.
- Operator's proposed cleaner→dirtier early exit policy,
  buzzer warning, direction evidence, or return hold
  exception. They need separate documented user approval.
- Native vendor Owen Logic .owle serialization, Save As,
  Compile, Simulation, physical communications and PLC
  programming.
- Hardware failure modes, manual emergency escape and
  independent fire release, which have priority over
  normal SCUD and PLC access policy.
